## Scaler routing

New pack builds route textures to HDCube3, maps and bitmap line art to Manga109Attempt, still images to Nomos8kSC, and animation frames through Manga109Attempt followed by HDCube Compact. The route matches each model to its training domain. No benchmark has confirmed the best model for each source. This checkout has no model weights or source export, so it cannot regenerate the enabled candidate with these routes.

## Approved art direction

Use detailed graphite linework, dry uneven hatching and warm illustrated materials. Preserve original poses, timing, crop and scene geometry. Style studies are references only; their plaque and handle differ from the original and must not replace those details. The original Room 215 sign has its own frame and separated digits, and the inspection view crops the lower part of the tall door.

The offline artwork job selects the Chapter 1 room archives, expressions referenced by the Chapter 1 scripts (including paired dialogue), opening and shared artwork. The selection is deliberately conservative and includes additional shared assets. Source files remain unchanged. Records are keyed by the original decoded pixels so unavailable assets fall back to the original.

Textures and other images use 4× dimensions. Character animation frames use 8× dimensions, or 1536×2048 for an original 192×256 frame. Game coordinates and hit masks remain in their original units. The runtime checks each enhanced image's size and SHA-256 before using it, and limits its decoded-image cache.

The earlier corridor-door overrides distorted the original crop, plaque and handle and were withdrawn. The current animation finish also does not reproduce the approved pencil treatment. Neither is ready for a Chapter 1 pack. Texture-only processing can be resumed with:
